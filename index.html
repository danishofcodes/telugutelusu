Word order that unlocks speaking

English: I eat rice. Telugu: Nenu annam tintunnanu — subject, object, verb last.

How Your Brain Learns Telugu Best

1. Chunks over grammar: Memorize "నాకు _ కావాలి" (I need _) and plug in words
2. Use మీరు when unsure: Formal/polite is always safe with strangers & elders
3. Context = memory: "అన్నం తిన్నావా?" sticks better than just "అన్నం"
4. SOV muscle memory: Always think "I + rice + eat" NOT "I eat rice"
5. Production > recognition: Try "build sentence" mode—active recall is 3x stronger!

1. Who is speaking?

Telugu changes with respect. Nuvvu = you (friends, younger). Meeru = you (elders, strangers, polite).

Sociolinguistic Tip

When in doubt, use మీరు (meeru)! Using informal నువ్వు (nuvvu) with elders/strangers is considered rude. But using formal with friends? Just extra polite. Safe choice = మీరు.
Real scenario: Meeting Telugu speaker? "మీరు ఎలా ఉన్నారు?" (formal) always works!

4. Glue words (postpositions)

They come after the noun: house-ki = to the house.

Common English Error

English speakers say "to home" → ❌ to ఇంటి
Telugu says "home-to" → ✓ ఇంటికి
Memory trick: Telugu "glues" the direction ONTO the noun: ఇల్లు (house) + కి (to) = ఇంటికి

Telugu Script & Pronunciation Guide

Vowels (16 total):
Short: అ (a-cup) ఇ (it) ఉ (put) ఋ (ri)
Long: ఆ (aah) ఈ (see) ఊ (food) ౠ (ree)
Unique Sounds: ఎ (e-bed) ఏ (ay-say) ఒ (o-go) ఓ (oh) ఐ (ai-aisle) ఔ (ou-house)

🎯 CRITICAL: Retroflex Sounds (tongue curls back to roof of mouth):
ట ఠ డ ఢ ణ ళ vs regular త థ ద ధ న ల
Try: "ఆట" (game) - curl tongue back. NOT like English T!
Minimal pairs: పట (city) vs పత (song), కడ (story) vs కద (to)

Aspirated (breathy): Add "h" sound: ఖ (kh) ఘ (gh) ఛ (ch) ఝ (jh) థ (th) ధ (dh) ఫ (ph) భ (bh)
Example: "భారతం" (India) - say "bh" like "clubhouse"
